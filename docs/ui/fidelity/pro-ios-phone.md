## Remaining differences

Capture: `/tmp/fidelity/settings-b/pro-ios.png` (full page: `pro-ios-long.png`). Design and app were compared side by side at 390 × 844 CSS px. Remaining differences are listed below.

- Subscription data supplies plan, status, renewal/end date, usage and license devices, but does not supply member-since or period-start dates. The app shows an em dash or “Until” rather than invented dates.
- The benefit checks and Active pill now follow the source; the remaining subscription dates and named device details are absent from the current response.
- The source has an iOS subscription-management note inside the hero card. The app note is below the license/device sections, leaving a visible card-height difference.
