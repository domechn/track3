# Summary desktop fidelity specification

Sources viewed: `desktop/analyze/summary-desktop.jpg`, `summary-empty-desktop.jpg`, `summary-loading-desktop.jpg`; authoritative HTML `ds/pages/analyze-summary*.html`, `analyze-summary.js`, `analyze-data.js`, `analyze.css`, `ds.css`, and `lib.js`. Measurements below are Chromium `getBoundingClientRect` and `getComputedStyle` at a 1600 × 1000 viewport, device scale 1.

The coordinates and example copy below describe the reference HTML. The app keeps its DatePicker, live total, refresh and sync controls in the heading area. The app reads dates, assets, currency and quote colours from the portfolio.

## Error and transient states

The design provides no error, open dialog, popover or toast image for Summary. Keep the six-card frame for a query error, show a concise error and Retry inside the failed card, retain last good sync time when available. Existing period detail, sync status popover, loading and sparse-month/unknown-profit explanations must remain reachable. Missing monthly snapshots stay grey dashes with their explanation. A partial month or year shows the known amount with the same sign tone and surface tint as a complete period, plus a question-mark badge in the card corner that explains which coins are excluded.

## Remaining differences

- The live banner splits the total change into market P&L, net funding, and other changes so the amounts reconcile; the static source combines funding and other changes as money added. The Positions note counts snapshots in the selected range as required by `fix_ui44.md`; the source sample lists up, down, and flat positions. The gain chart line follows the product's green gain color, while the static source uses blue.
- UI24 re-rendered `ds/pages/analyze-summary.html` on 7 Nov 2026 at 1280 × 1000 and 1920 × 1000 (`/tmp/ui24-current/source-{1280,1920}.png`), then compared fresh dark app captures (`/tmp/ui24-current/app-{1280,1920}.png`). With the live 49 px toolbar aligned out, first-viewport pixel differences are **12.696%** and **11.186%** respectively (RGB-sum threshold 48). At 1280 px the app stacks Summary panels so the complete amounts and labels remain readable; the source keeps narrow side-by-side panels and clips some values. At 1920 px the app uses the shared centered 1488 px content width and a 10 px scroll gutter, while the static source anchors its grid at x=84. These product shell and readable-value requirements account for the remaining page-level difference.
- The 1920 px source month card measures 181.45 × 167 px; the app's first card is about 179.6 × 167 px. Its isolated pixel difference is **1.589%**. The full month grid difference is **6.320%** because the scroll gutter makes later columns progressively shift horizontally; the card content and height are aligned. The source shows two years without a pager. A 12-year history shows four per page in the app; when only three are available, the fourth row's reserved space keeps the panel height steady. The source has no matching long-history state. The centered shared content width also leaves visible page space beside By year at 1920 px.
- Compared the source HTML and fresh app capture on 8 Nov 2026 at 1600 px. With the app's 49 px product toolbar aligned out, the first viewport differs by **9.816%** (RGB sum threshold 48). The app keeps its live toolbar and a scroll host with a 10 px gutter; its P&L explanation control also adds height before the month cards. Those controls have live actions that the static source page does not represent. Source: `/tmp/fidelity-desktop/source-final/analyze-summary.png`; app: `/tmp/fidelity-desktop/summary/summary-full-desktop.png`.
- The same aligned 8 Nov comparison gives **2.924%** for one snapshot and **0.898%** for loading (RGB sum threshold 48): `/tmp/fidelity-desktop/source-final/analyze-summary-{empty,loading}.png` versus `/tmp/fidelity-desktop/summary/summary-{empty,loading}-desktop.png`.
- The isolated **Profit attribution** card differs by **8.22%** over the common 977 × 588 px area. Its rows are 52 px instead of the source's 48 px, bars are 16 px instead of 8 px, and the numeric grid reserves enough width for whole CNY amounts. These sizes are required by `fix_ui13.md` to prevent sign wrapping and cramped rows. The **Top profit and loss coins** card differs by **2.66%** over 481 × 588 px; the amount column grows to fit large currency values. Source and app card captures are in `/tmp/ui13-design-desktop-*.png` and `/tmp/ui13-app-desktop-*.png`.
- Before the attribution row changes, a 5 Nov 2026 populated source render and app capture differed by **12.14%** over the 1600 × 1000 viewport. Aligning the content below the app's fixed 49 px product toolbar and expanding the capture scroll host gave **0.53%** across the 1600 × 1621 source page (RGB sum threshold 48). The source has no live total, date, refresh or sync toolbar. The current two-peak mark and Settings cog also differ from the source tile and sun. Images and measured bounds are in `/tmp/ui3-remeasure/`.
- When a chart has no data, it shows the shared 200 px empty state with an icon and refresh hint. The populated source screens have no matching no-chart view.
- With 47 attribution groups, the product adds ten-row pagination. The source has eight groups, so its populated image has no pager to compare. The total still covers all groups, and the contributor and detractor cards retain their full data.
- The header keeps the live total, DatePicker, refresh control, and sync-status popover because those existing controls provide portfolio actions absent from the static design page. `Updated 09:41` describes a local refresh; the source's `Synced 09:41` would incorrectly imply S3 synchronization.
- The one-snapshot attribution caption starts on the real first snapshot, 31 Oct 2026. The source says 1 Jul 2025 despite that snapshot being absent from its empty data.
- The attribution date has a small disclosure chevron and a screen-reader name for its coin/source/wallet/asset-type grouping menu. The source shows the date alone. An `Unexplained` column appears only when recorded market and quantity effects leave a residual, so the total remains accountable; the design sample has no residual.
- Histories over 60 month endpoints use a bounded sample because `calculateTotalProfit` accepts one range per call. The chart then omits the Best month annotation when it cannot identify adjacent months. A batched query would need a middlelayer change outside this task's allowed paths.
