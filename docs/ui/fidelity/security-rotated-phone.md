## Remaining differences

Capture: `/tmp/fidelity/settings-b/security-rotated.png` (full page: `security-rotated-long.png`). Design and app were compared side by side at 390 × 844 CSS px. Remaining differences are listed below.

- The app does not store an encryption key ID, creation/rotation times, or per-category secret counts. Dashes and truthful encryption labels replace invented values.
- The approved Recovery card refers to a local encryption recovery key. The product offers a sync recovery file only after sync unlock; the two are not interchangeable.
- The approved app-lock timeout and launch timing controls are unavailable. The existing iOS biometric lock switch remains interactive.
- Card heights and category rows differ because the source presents unavailable key metadata, secret counts, and a recovery-key flow. The supported iOS biometric lock controls use the current product illustration.
- The app now shows the new-key-active banner and rotation toast. Their details say only that saved secrets were re-encrypted: the product cannot verify the source's five-secret count or export a local encryption recovery key.
