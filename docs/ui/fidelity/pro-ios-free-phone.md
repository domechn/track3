## Remaining differences

Capture: `/tmp/fidelity/settings-b/pro-ios-free.png` (full page: `pro-ios-free-long.png`). Design and app were compared side by side at 390 × 844 CSS px. Remaining differences are listed below.

- Subscription data supplies plan, status, renewal/end date, usage and license devices, but does not supply member-since or period-start dates. The app shows an em dash or “Until” rather than invented dates.
- The benefit locks and Free pill now follow the source. The source's Subscribe and Compare plans actions are absent because the iOS app only supports license activation here.
- The source has separate restore/manage buttons and a purchase-unavailable note in the hero card. The app keeps license entry and a note below its cards; order and geometry differ.
