## Remaining differences

- The app retains a visible Market price mode alongside Manual and Follow because existing custom assets support market pricing and the existing add-asset flow exposes it. The approved mockup has only Manual and Follow.
- The price segment is wider because the supported Market option remains visible. The native source selector uses the platform chevron and keeps its keyboard and screen-reader behavior. Minor icon strokes and text rasterization differ.
