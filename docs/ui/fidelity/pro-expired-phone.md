## Remaining differences

Capture: `/tmp/fidelity/settings-b/pro-expired.png` (full page: `pro-expired-long.png`). Design and app were compared side by side at 390 × 844 CSS px. Remaining differences are listed below.

- Subscription data supplies plan, status, renewal/end date, usage and license devices, but does not supply member-since or period-start dates. The app shows an em dash or “Until” rather than invented dates.
- The warning now uses the reported expiry date, paused-feature copy, and source warning geometry; the Expired pill is red and the billing row says “not renewed”. The source's Subscribe again action is unavailable in the iOS purchase flow.
- The source includes historical member and last-period dates and an expired-purchase flow. The API provides only period end; the app can show expiration but not those extra dates or iOS purchase actions.
