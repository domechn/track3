## Remaining differences

Capture: `/tmp/fidelity/settings-b/pro-invalid.png` (full page: `pro-invalid-long.png`). Design and app were compared side by side at 390 × 844 CSS px. Remaining differences are listed below.

- Subscription data supplies plan, status, renewal/end date, usage and license devices, but does not supply member-since or period-start dates. The app shows an em dash or “Until” rather than invented dates.
- The benefit locks and Free status now follow the source. The source's Subscribe and Compare plans actions are absent because the iOS app only supports license activation here.
- The source shows an active license plus an invalid replacement key. The product read-only active key field cannot enter this state; the capture tests invalid activation from Free and shows a standard toast.
- The capture now starts at the top of the page after the Verify action. The standard toast overlays the heading, unlike the approved image.
