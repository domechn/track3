Track3 Pro activates automatically after checkout. This usually takes a few seconds.

  1. Switch back to the Track3 app (desktop or iPhone/iPad).
  2. Open System Info (or More → Settings → Pro on mobile).
  3. Your Pro status refreshes automatically. If it still shows Free after a minute, quit and reopen the app.

To use Pro on another device, open Track3 → System Info (or More → Settings → Pro on iPhone/iPad) and enter your license key. You can use it on up to 3 devices. Keep it safe: anyone with the key can use it.

You can manage or cancel the subscription any time from the same screen.

Trouble? Open an issue and include the email on your Stripe receipt.
