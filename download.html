iPhone & iPad

Build and sideload the iOS app.

The app is not on the App Store. These community steps use a Mac and a personal Apple ID; they have not been checked on a physical device.

  1. Install Node 24 and Tauri’s iOS prerequisites on a Mac. Clone the Track3 repository and run corepack yarn.
  2. Run corepack yarn tauri ios dev --open. In Xcode, choose the Track3 iOS target and your Apple ID team under Signing & Capabilities. Change the bundle ID if needed, select your connected device, and press Run. An IPA can also be signed and installed with AltStore or Sideloadly.
  3. With a free Apple ID, renew signing about every 7 days.

See the iOS overview and release and signing notes.
