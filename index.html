03 / PRIVACY

Built around local control.

Local database

Portfolio records stay in a local SQLite database on your device.

Read-only credentials

Use read-only exchange keys. Never grant trade or withdrawal permission.

Optional encrypted sync

Sync through S3 storage you configure, with end-to-end encryption.

How data and services work

Refreshes contact your sources, assistant requests reach your chosen provider, and optional sync uses your S3 storage. Crypto prices and public wallet balances may come from public APIs such as CoinGecko. Currency rates, the stablecoin list, stock prices, Pro refreshes, and license checks use Track3's services. Pro wallet refreshes send wallet addresses to Track3's service, and Track3 service requests include a persistent client ID.

Sensitive configuration and chat messages are encrypted before storage; chat titles and previews are not. Portfolio records are not app-encrypted. On desktop, set your own encryption key for stronger protection. Only public on-chain wallet data is read; no wallet signatures are needed.

The desktop and iOS apps send usage events for launches, refreshes, and subscription-button clicks to Aptabase at eu.aptabase.com. There is no in-app opt-out. Events carry a persistent client ID and session ID, plus details such as OS, user agent, locale, app and Tauri versions; refresh events may include source IDs. Privacy details
