What it does

A calmer command center for your portfolio.

Your portfolio database stays on your device. Connect with read-only credentials, refresh when you want, and see where every balance sits.

Your whole history, one tap away.

Every refresh becomes a snapshot. Scroll back through days and months, open any day, and compare two periods side by side.

  • Daily snapshots with change and asset counts
  • Compare any two dates or ranges
  • Per-exchange and per-wallet views

Know what you actually made.

Realized and unrealized PnL, cost basis with your chosen method, and reports with CSV and JSON export — not just a balance.

  • FIFO and other cost-basis methods
  • Profit attribution by asset
  • Goals and alerts with notifications

Ask your portfolio anything.

“How much BTC do I hold?” “What moved most this month?” The assistant answers from your local data using the provider you choose; chats are stored encrypted.

  • Natural-language questions on holdings and trends
  • Proposed changes always need your approval
  • Rebalancing suggestions from your targets

Privacy

Your data, your control.

Portfolio records stay in a local SQLite database. Refreshes contact your sources, assistant requests reach your chosen provider, and optional sync uses your S3 storage.

Read-only keysExchange API keys without trade or withdrawal permission.
Encrypted storageSensitive configuration and chat content are encrypted before storage. Portfolio records are not app-encrypted.
No wallet signaturesOnly public on-chain data is read. Nothing to approve.
Open sourceRead the code on GitHub. Export and back up your data when you choose.

Common questions

Before you start.

Which desktop platforms work?

Releases provide macOS, Windows and Linux installers. The download page picks a recommended package and lists every available format.

Is the iOS app on the App Store?

No. Build and sign it from the repository on a Mac. See the iOS install steps and signing notes.

Are all records encrypted?

Portfolio assets and transactions use a local SQLite database and are not encrypted by the app. Credentials, wallet addresses and chats are encrypted before storage. Privacy details

Can I move data between devices?

Use file import and export, a backup folder such as iCloud Drive, or optional encrypted sync with S3 storage you configure.
